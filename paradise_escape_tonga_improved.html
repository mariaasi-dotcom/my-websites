Good to know

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Tonga?

The dry season, from May to October, is often popular for cooler weather and marine activities. Whale-watching availability is seasonal, so confirm dates with a licensed operator.

Do I need to book activities in advance?

Yes. Booking early is recommended, especially for whale experiences, island-hopping and guided cultural tours.

How can I travel responsibly in Tonga?

Respect local customs, use less plastic, protect reefs and wildlife, conserve water, and choose locally owned businesses.
